The Hadith

Reports of what the Prophet ﷺ said, did, and approved — collected, chain-verified, and graded by Muslim scholars over the first three centuries.

How hadith are graded

Not every report carries the same weight. Scholars evaluate the chain of narrators (who transmitted it) and the text (is it consistent with other evidence) and assign a grade:

SahihAuthentic — unbroken chain of reliable narrators.
HasanGood — reliable but with a minor issue in the chain.
DaifWeak — a flaw in the chain or text.
MawduFabricated — rejected outright.
