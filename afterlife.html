الآخِرَة

The Afterlife

Resurrection, judgment, paradise, hell. Dominant in the short surahs at the end of the Quran (Juz ʿAmma).

A skeleton selection — more verses to come.

1The Hour
Signs of the end of the world.
2Death & the grave
The barzakh — intermediate state.
3Resurrection
The trumpet sounds; all are raised.
4Judgment & the scale
Records and weighed deeds.
5Paradise (Jannah)
The garden of reward.
6Hell (Jahannam)
Fire of consequence.
The Hour
The Hour
يَا أَيُّهَا النَّاسُ اتَّقُوا رَبَّكُمْ ۚ إِنَّ زَلْزَلَةَ السَّاعَةِ شَيْءٌ عَظِيمٌ
"O people, fear your Lord — indeed, the convulsion of the Hour is a tremendous thing."
Quran 22:1
Signs
إِذَا الشَّمْسُ كُوِّرَتْ ۝ وَإِذَا النُّجُومُ انكَدَرَتْ
"When the sun is folded up, and when the stars fall scattered…"
Quran 81:1–2
Death & the grave
The barzakh
وَمِن وَرَائِهِم بَرْزَخٌ إِلَىٰ يَوْمِ يُبْعَثُونَ
"And before them is a barrier [barzakh] until the Day they are resurrected."
Quran 23:100
Every soul
كُلُّ نَفْسٍ ذَائِقَةُ الْمَوْتِ
"Every soul will taste death."
Quran 3:185
Resurrection
The trumpet
وَنُفِخَ فِي الصُّورِ فَإِذَا هُم مِّنَ الْأَجْدَاثِ إِلَىٰ رَبِّهِمْ يَنسِلُونَ
"The trumpet will be blown, and at once from the graves to their Lord they will run."
Quran 36:51
Bodily
أَيَحْسَبُ الْإِنسَانُ أَلَّن نَّجْمَعَ عِظَامَهُ ۝ بَلَىٰ قَادِرِينَ عَلَىٰ أَن نُّسَوِّيَ بَنَانَهُ
"Does man think We will not assemble his bones? Yes — We are able to put together his very fingertips."
Quran 75:3–4
Judgment & the scale
The record
اقْرَأْ كِتَابَكَ كَفَىٰ بِنَفْسِكَ الْيَوْمَ عَلَيْكَ حَسِيبًا
"Read your record — sufficient is yourself against you this Day as accountant."
Quran 17:14
The scale
فَمَن يَعْمَلْ مِثْقَالَ ذَرَّةٍ خَيْرًا يَرَهُ ۝ وَمَن يَعْمَلْ مِثْقَالَ ذَرَّةٍ شَرًّا يَرَهُ
"Whoever does an atom's weight of good will see it; and whoever does an atom's weight of evil will see it."
Quran 99:7–8
Paradise (Jannah)
The reward
إِنَّ الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا وَعَمِلُوا الصَّالِحَاتِ كَانَتْ لَهُمْ جَنَّاتُ الْفِرْدَوْسِ نُزُلًا
"Indeed, those who believe and do righteous deeds — the Gardens of Paradise will be their lodging."
Quran 18:107
Rivers
مَّثَلُ الْجَنَّةِ الَّتِي وُعِدَ الْمُتَّقُونَ ۖ تَجْرِي مِن تَحْتِهَا الْأَنْهَارُ
"The likeness of the Garden promised to the God-conscious — rivers flow beneath it."
Quran 13:35
Peace
لَا يَسْمَعُونَ فِيهَا لَغْوًا وَلَا تَأْثِيمًا ۝ إِلَّا قِيلًا سَلَامًا سَلَامًا
"They will hear no idle talk there, nor any sinful speech — only the greeting 'Peace, peace.'"
Quran 56:25–26
Beyond imagining
فَلَا تَعْلَمُ نَفْسٌ مَّا أُخْفِيَ لَهُم مِّن قُرَّةِ أَعْيُنٍ جَزَاءً بِمَا كَانُوا يَعْمَلُونَ
"No soul knows what has been hidden for them of comfort to the eyes — as reward for what they used to do."
Quran 32:17
Hell (Jahannam)
Consequence
إِنَّ الَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا بِآيَاتِنَا سَوْفَ نُصْلِيهِمْ نَارًا
"Indeed, those who disbelieve in Our signs — We will drive them into a Fire…"
Quran 4:56
Regret
يَا لَيْتَنِي قَدَّمْتُ لِحَيَاتِي
"They will say: 'If only I had sent ahead something for my life.'"
Quran 89:24
رَبَّنَا آتِنَا فِي الدُّنْيَا حَسَنَةً وَفِي الْآخِرَةِ حَسَنَةً

"Our Lord, grant us good in this world and good in the next." — Quran 2:201
