آدَم ﷺ

Ādam

The first man. The first prophet. The first repentance.
● 9 scenes ● ~8 min read ● Q 2 · 7 · 15 · 17 · 20 · 38
Key Takeaways

If you remember nothing else

1
Humans are the species defined by the capacity to know. Allah taught Adam the names — that, not strength, is the gift the angels could not match.
2
Pride is the original sin. Iblīs did not deny God — he ranked himself above God's command. The first refusal in creation is a calculation of self-worth.
3
The test is a single boundary inside abundance, not deprivation. The Garden was free except for one tree — the moral shape of every test that follows.
4
Adam and Iblīs both disobeyed. One was forgiven; one was cast away. The difference: Adam owned it. Iblīs argued. The model of repentance is ownership without excuse.
5
There is no inherited guilt. Adam's mistake is Adam's. Every soul is born clean and answers for its own choices — the doctrine of fiṭra.
6
Killing one soul is killing all of humanity. The Quran's verdict on the first murder becomes its foundational verse on the sanctity of human life.
